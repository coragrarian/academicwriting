About Academic Writing for Agrarian Sciences

The research, corpus and teaching work behind this resource.

About the resource

Academic Writing for Agrarian Sciences is an interactive self-study resource for undergraduate and postgraduate students and researchers writing research articles in English. The available modules cover the Introduction, Methods and Results sections.

Guided exercises let you practise the structure and language of academic writing, check your answers and review feedback. Responses and progress are saved locally in your browser, so you can return to your work and continue at your own pace.

Research behind the resource

The research behind the resource investigates lexical and syntactic features of academic English in the Agrarian Sciences and their relationship to rhetorical functions. It examines how language used in disciplinary texts can inform materials for teaching academic writing.

The work addresses the academic English needs of Brazilian students and researchers, connecting linguistic analysis with pedagogical applications.

CorAgrarian

CorAgrarian is a specialised corpus of English-language journal articles in the Agrarian Sciences. It supplies disciplinary research data for examining how academic English is used in published research.

CorAgrarian and this website serve different purposes: the corpus provides texts for research; the website presents related learning activities.

From research to learning

Project documentation connects the analysis of disciplinary writing with the development of teaching materials for classroom use and independent online study. Research into the language and rhetorical organisation of research articles informs that pedagogical work.

On this site, the activities take the form of guided practice with the structure and language of research articles. Learners examine choices in context, check responses and use feedback to revisit their answers.
