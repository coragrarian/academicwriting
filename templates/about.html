About Academic Writing for Agrarian Sciences

About the resource

This website provides interactive self-study activities focused on academic writing in research articles in the Agrarian Sciences. The available modules cover the Introduction, Methods and Results sections.

Guided exercises let you check your answers and review feedback. Responses and progress are saved locally in your browser, so you can return to your work and continue at your own pace.

Research context

The resource was developed in connection with the research project Escrita acadêmica em língua inglesa nas ciências agrárias: necessidades e insumos para aplicações pedagógicas.

Research on academic writing in the Agrarian Sciences informs the pedagogical activities presented here.
