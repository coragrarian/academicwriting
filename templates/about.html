About Academic Writing for Agrarian Sciences

Research on academic English in the Agrarian Sciences, corpus data and teaching materials.

Academic Writing for Agrarian Sciences is an interactive self-study resource for undergraduate and postgraduate students and researchers writing research articles in English. The available modules cover the Introduction, Methods and Results sections.

Guided exercises let you practise the structure and language of academic writing, check your answers and review feedback. Responses and progress are saved locally in your browser, so you can return to your work and continue at your own pace.

The research project

The project investigates lexical and syntactic features of academic English in the Agrarian Sciences and their relationship to rhetorical functions. It examines how language used in disciplinary texts can inform materials for teaching academic writing.

The work addresses the academic English needs of Brazilian students and researchers, connecting linguistic analysis with pedagogical applications.

CorAgrarian

CorAgrarian is a specialised corpus of English-language journal articles in the Agrarian Sciences. It supplies disciplinary research data for examining how academic English is used in published research.

CorAgrarian provides texts for research; this website presents related learning activities.

Pedagogical materials

Project documentation connects disciplinary linguistic research with the development and adaptation of teaching materials for classroom use and independent online study. Analysis of the language and rhetorical organisation of research articles informs that pedagogical work.

This site presents the activities as interactive self-study material. Learners examine choices in context, check responses and use feedback to revisit their answers.
