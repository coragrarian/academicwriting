The CorAgrarian corpus

CorAgrarian is a specialised corpus of English-language research articles published in high-impact journals in the Agrarian Sciences and one of the empirical resources informing this course. Corpus evidence is used alongside published research examples and academic-writing scholarship throughout the materials.
