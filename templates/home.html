The research project

Research into academic English in the Agrarian Sciences examines lexical and syntactic features and their relationship to rhetorical functions in research articles.

CorAgrarian supplies disciplinary corpus data for this work. The research informs pedagogical materials for academic writing, presented here as interactive self-study activities.
