Academic Writing for Agrarian Sciences

Academic Writing for Agrarian Sciences is an interactive self-study resource for students and researchers in the Agrarian Sciences. Working section by section through research articles, it helps learners recognise disciplinary rhetorical conventions and practise the grammar, vocabulary and phraseology used to realise them. The materials are informed by the CorAgrarian corpus, published Agrarian Sciences research and academic-writing scholarship.

The CorAgrarian corpus

CorAgrarian is a specialised corpus of academic writing in the Agrarian Sciences and one of the empirical resources informing this course. Corpus evidence is used alongside published research examples and academic-writing scholarship throughout the materials.
